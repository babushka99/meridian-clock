# Privacy Policy — Meridian Clock

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

Meridian Clock does not collect, send, sell or share any data. Ever.

## What the extension stores

Only your own settings, kept **on your device** in Chrome's local extension storage
(`chrome.storage.local`):

- the cities you saved and which one is your home city
- 12-hour or 24-hour time
- dark or light theme
- which map labels are switched on or off

This never leaves your computer. Removing the extension deletes it.

## What the extension does not do

- No accounts, sign-in, analytics, tracking, cookies or ads.
- No network requests at all. The page's content security policy sets `connect-src 'none'`, so
  the browser itself blocks any attempt to reach the internet.
- No access to your browsing history, tabs, page contents or location. The only permission it
  asks for is `storage`.

All city data, maps and fonts are bundled inside the extension.
